Δ The mark

A stroked delta on a 64-unit grid, stroke 6, mitred. Pick the variant by background; never recolour the file. On the web it is inline SVG and inherits currentColor.

Delta — teal

assets/logo/gst-delta-icon-teal-stroke-thick.svg

Use: the default. Any light or near-black background. Navigation, documents, decks, decoration.

Not on teal or mid-tone/busy backgrounds.

SVG

Delta — ink

assets/logo/gst-delta-icon-dark-stroke-thick.svg

Use: single-colour print, engraving, documents where teal is unavailable, or on a teal block when white is too bright.

Not on dark backgrounds.

SVG

Delta — white

assets/logo/gst-delta-icon-white-stroke-thick.svg

Use: over photography (dark regions), teal blocks, dark merchandise.

Invisible on white — never on light surfaces.

Δ The wordmark

The site's header lockup: delta at 1 em, GST in GST Mono 900 with −0.05 em tracking and a teal S, then an 8 px teal square. Minimum height 24 px. Clear space: half the cap height on all sides.

Wordmark

assets/wordmark/gst-wordmark-on-{light,dark}.svg

Use: decks, documents, email footers, partner listings — wherever the site header would appear. The font is embedded in the SVG; use the @2x.png where SVG fonts are not honoured (Office, some CMSs).

Do not rebuild it from type; do not drop the dot or the teal S; do not set it under 24 px tall.

Δ Rules in one breath

DoDon't
Pick the mark variant for the backgroundRecolour a file, or put teal on teal / white on white
Keep the stroke, mitred joins and proportionsFill the triangle, round it, rotate, skew, add shadows or gradients
Set GST in GST Mono 900 with the teal S and the dotRetype the wordmark in another face or weight
Give the mark clear space of 2× its stroke widthCrowd it with text, rules or other logos
